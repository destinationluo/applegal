Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StagePulse ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the StagePulse mobile application (the "App").

Key Commitment: StagePulse analyzes beat timing locally on your device. We do not record, store, or upload your audio, and we do not require an account.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

StagePulse does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or other personally identifiable information.

We do not operate servers that receive your microphone audio, target BPM settings, practice sessions, or usage history.

2. Microphone and Audio Processing

StagePulse uses microphone access only to estimate live tempo and beat timing. Audio input is processed in real time on your device to calculate BPM and confidence. The App does not save audio files, create recordings, or upload microphone data to us or to a cloud service.

3. Local App Data

The App may store simple settings locally on your device, such as your target BPM, recent target values, purchase entitlement state, and app preferences. You can remove this local data by deleting the App from your device.

4. Advertising

The free version of StagePulse may display a banner ad through Google AdMob. AdMob may process device, advertising, and usage signals according to Google's own policies and the consent choices available on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

5. In-App Purchases

StagePulse offers a one-time buyout option to remove ads and unlock the Extreme Blackout mode. Purchases, billing, refunds, and payment information are handled by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your credit card information.

6. Children's Privacy

StagePulse is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
