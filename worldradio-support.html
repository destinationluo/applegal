World Radio Support

Help with listening, Pro Lifetime, and CarPlay.

Need a hand? Email moguiris133@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

A station will not play

Check your internet connection and try another station. Individual broadcasters may be offline or unavailable in your region. World Radio streams live audio and does not offer offline downloads.

If the issue affects one station, include its name when you contact us. If several stations are affected, tell us whether you are using Wi-Fi or mobile data. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment-card details.

Restore Pro Lifetime

Open World Radio → Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that purchased Pro. Apple may ask you to authenticate. Restoration recovers eligible purchase access; it does not recover deleted favorites or listening history.

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no subscription. For an App Store refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem.

Your seven-day CarPlay trial

Open Settings → CarPlay to claim the free trial or view its status. It unlocks CarPlay only for seven consecutive days from your original trial transaction. Restoring or reinstalling does not restart the trial.

There is no automatic renewal or charge. After the trial, you can choose to buy Pro Lifetime to keep using CarPlay. Listening on your iPhone remains free.

Connect to CarPlay

CarPlay requires a compatible vehicle or stereo, an internet connection, and an active trial or Pro Lifetime. Set up your stations on your iPhone before driving. Manage purchases while safely parked and disconnected from CarPlay.

If connection fails, check the vehicle’s connection instructions and your iPhone’s CarPlay settings. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, vehicle or stereo model, and whether you use a cable or wireless connection.

Favorites and listening history

Your favorites and recent listening are stored on your iPhone. Favorite sync is currently unavailable. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting unless you have a suitable backup and understand that local data may be lost.

You can clear recent listening separately in Settings → Data & privacy. Free access includes up to five favorites; Pro Lifetime allows unlimited favorites.

Report a listing or rights concern

Email moguiris133@gmail.com with the station name or identifier and a description of the issue. For a rights concern, include your contact information and the basis for your request. We can review the listing but do not control independent broadcaster streams.

For details about data handling and use of the app, see our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
