Aurora Opportunity Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Aurora Opportunity helps you check aurora and cloud forecasts for saved places in the United States. Saved places and reminders stay on your iPhone. The app connects directly to Apple and NOAA for search, forecasts, and purchases; it does not require an app account or use a developer-operated forecast backend.

1. Who We Are

Aurora Opportunity is provided by the developer identified on its App Store listing. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to that developer. For privacy questions or requests, email moguiris133@gmail.com.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores saved city or region names, coordinates rounded to 0.01°, time zones, private place names, your selected place, cached forecast results, reminder dates, preferences, and purchase entitlement state in its local app storage. Purchase state can include transaction identifiers, purchase dates, expiration dates, and revocation status. Any retained in-app feedback or history is also local.

Private place names are not included in the app’s search or forecast requests. We do not operate an account database, cloud sync service, or server that receives your saved places, private names, reminders, or purchase transaction records.

3. Search and Forecast Requests

  • Apple Maps: Text you enter in place search is sent to Apple through MapKit to find matching places. Search text may contain any address or other information you choose to type. The app does not request device location permission or track your GPS position.
  • Apple Weather: The selected saved place’s coordinates, rounded to 0.01°, are sent through WeatherKit to retrieve current weather and hourly cloud forecasts. A saved place is not necessarily your current location.
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center: The app downloads a public aurora forecast grid over HTTPS and finds the nearby grid point on your device. The app does not include your selected place’s coordinates, search text, or private name in the NOAA request.

Network providers necessarily receive connection information such as your IP address and may process request metadata under their own policies. Rounding coordinates does not make a network request anonymous. These connections are used when you search or refresh forecasts; the app does not continuously monitor aurora conditions in the background.

See the Apple Privacy Policy and NOAA Privacy Policy for their handling of information.

4. Local Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks iOS for notification permission and schedules a one-time notification on your device. A reminder contains the saved place’s city or region name and a local place identifier so tapping it can open the associated forecast. It does not include your private place name.

Notification text may appear on your lock screen or other notification surfaces according to your Apple device settings. You can cancel reminders in the app and change permission or preview visibility in iOS Settings. This edition does not register a remote push token with a developer server or send reminders from a cloud monitoring service.

5. Purchases and Apple Services

Apple processes Annual Pro subscriptions and 14-Day Trip Pass purchases through the App Store. StoreKit verifies purchase transactions on your device so the app can determine access. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide developers with sales reports and diagnostics through its platform, subject to Apple’s policies and your sharing settings.

The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

6. Support and This Website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records, or meet legal obligations; you can contact us to request deletion.

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening them sends normal web connection information to GitHub. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies to these pages. GitHub handles hosting information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

7. Retention, Deletion, and Your Choices

Saved app content remains on your device until you remove it. In Settings, Delete all my data clears saved places, private names, cached forecast records, local history, and scheduled reminders from this installation. The app keeps a local reset preference to prevent automatic purchase restoration until you explicitly restore or purchase again. Deleting the app removes its local storage; offloading the app may retain it.

Deleting app data does not cancel an App Store subscription, erase Apple’s purchase records, or delete information held independently by Apple, NOAA, GitHub, or your email provider. Device or iCloud backups may contain app data depending on your settings; manage those copies through Apple’s backup controls. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted places or reminders.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information we hold, or complain to your local privacy authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. We cannot retrieve saved place data that exists only on your device.

8. Security and International Processing

The app uses iOS app storage protections and encrypted network connections for its requests. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Apple, NOAA, GitHub, and email providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app, service providers, or legal requirements. We will post the revised policy with an updated date and provide additional notice where required. Questions: moguiris133@gmail.com.
