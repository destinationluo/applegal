Support

Spirit Box & EVP Recorder

Need help with playback, recordings, or Pro? Email moguiris133@gmail.com. Please describe what happened and your iPhone and iOS versions. Send recordings only if you choose to share them.

No sound

Raise your iPhone's media volume and check whether sound is going to its speaker, headphones, or a Bluetooth device. In the app, open Settings → Help & support → Play test sound. If you have just disconnected headphones or ended a call, return to the session and resume it yourself.

Microphone or missing environment track

Check microphone access in iOS Settings for this app. The microphone records your surroundings; the app's generated audio is saved separately. If access is denied, you can choose app-audio-only recording. A track that was not recorded cannot be recovered later.

Recordings and export

Open History to replay saved sessions. Free access includes playback of existing recordings and export of an available environment track. Pro adds app-audio, mixed-audio, and session-data exports. Recordings are stored on your device; export important ones before deleting the app or moving to a new iPhone. Deleting a recording does not restore a used free session.

Pro purchase and restore

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. On an iPhone using the Apple Account that purchased it, open Settings → Restore purchases. If Apple cannot connect, try again later. Your existing verified access and recordings are not removed by a connection failure. For purchase or refund issues that the app cannot resolve, contact Apple's purchase support.

Privacy and use

The app is for entertainment. Generated sounds and magnetic readings do not verify paranormal activity. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for more detail.
