Privacy Policy

World Radio

Effective date and last updated: October 21, 2026

World Radio lets you discover and listen to live radio without an app account. Favorites and listening history stay on your iPhone. Listening and online searches connect to external services, which receive the network information needed to respond.

1. Who we are

World Radio (the “App”) is provided by the developer identified on its App Store listing. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to that developer. For privacy questions or requests, email moguiris133@gmail.com.

2. Information on your iPhone

The App stores favorite stations, their order and saved removals, recent listening, preferences, station listings, and purchase-access information on your device. These support playback, search, your library, and Pro access. A randomly generated local identifier helps keep favorite changes consistent; it is not an advertising identifier.

The App does not request your precise device location, microphone, camera, contacts, or photos. Searching for a city uses the place you enter, not your current location. World Radio does not record or download broadcasts for offline listening.

The current public App does not include advertising or analytics services, use an advertising identifier, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information. Advertising within a station’s broadcast is controlled by that broadcaster.

3. Searches, streams, and external services

  • Radio Browser: Online station searches and list updates send search terms, filters, pagination requests, or a station identifier to Radio Browser servers. They also receive normal network information such as your IP address and the App’s request information. See Radio Browser.
  • Apple Maps: City searches first use the city list stored in the App. If there is no match, the search text may be sent to Apple Maps to find a city. This does not request your device location. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Broadcasters and stream providers: Listening connects directly to the selected station’s service. That service receives your IP address and stream-request information and can observe connection times. Playback can continue while the screen is locked or the App is in the background; pause playback to stop the stream.
  • Links: A station website opens only when you choose it. Websites and services you visit handle information under their own policies.

We do not operate a server that receives your favorite list, listening history, or audio. External providers may process requests in other countries. Their network logs and retention practices are controlled by those providers; clearing the App’s local history does not delete their logs.

4. Apple purchases and diagnostics

Apple processes Pro Lifetime purchases, billing, refunds, and restoration. The App checks Apple-provided purchase information, including purchase and revocation status, to enable Pro. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Restoring a purchase does not restore your favorites or listening history.

Apple may provide sales reports and diagnostics according to its platform policies and your sharing settings. Local diagnostic information may also be kept by the App or iOS for troubleshooting. The App does not automatically upload these local logs to a developer server.

5. Favorite sync and device backups

Favorite sync is currently unavailable in the public App. Your favorites remain on this iPhone. Where a version makes sync available and you choose to enable it with Pro, favorites, their order, and saved removals are shared through your private iCloud account. Listening history, playback, and volume do not sync.

Turning sync off keeps existing copies. Where available, Delete cloud favorites turns sync off and removes the iCloud copy while retaining favorites on this iPhone; removals also reach other devices that continue syncing. Your App Store purchase account and iCloud account are separate.

Apple device backups or device transfers may include local App data, depending on your settings. Those copies are separate from in-app favorite sync and must be managed through Apple’s settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Favorites and preferences remain until you change or remove them or delete the App’s data. Removing a favorite hides it from your list; a saved removal entry may remain to prevent it from reappearing if favorites are later merged. Recent listening is kept as a limited list and can be cleared in Settings → Data & privacy. The station cache can be cleared separately and may be refreshed as you use the App.

Deleting the App removes its local storage; offloading it may retain data. Device backups, copies shared elsewhere, any iCloud favorites, and Apple’s purchase records must be managed separately. Clearing history or deleting the App does not request a refund. We cannot recover data stored only on your device.

7. Feedback and privacy requests

Help & feedback lets you preview a report with the app version, iOS version, issue category, your comments, and—only if selected—the current station identifier and playback state. No report is sent automatically. You choose a destination in the system share sheet. The App does not automatically attach stream addresses, listening history, your location, or account credentials.

If you email us, we receive your address, message, and anything you choose to attach. We use this to answer your request, investigate issues, and meet applicable obligations. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. We keep it only as needed for these purposes and applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending passwords or payment details.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information we hold, such as support emails, and may contact your local privacy authority. Email the address above. We cannot access or delete information held only on your device or independently by a broadcaster.

8. This website, children, and security

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. GitHub receives normal web requests and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data-collection information.

World Radio is a general-audience radio app, not a service directed to children. Broadcast content varies by station. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. The App uses iOS storage protections; no storage or network connection can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes

We will update this page when data practices change and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required. The date above identifies this version. See also the Terms of Use.
