NOAA’s nearby grid percentage is a model value, not a personalized probability that you will see an aurora. Short-term aurora forecasts do not predict an entire evening, and cloud forecasts do not guarantee clear skies.

The app does not provide emergency warnings, navigation, road-condition advice, or a determination that travel or outdoor activity is safe. Review official weather alerts, local conditions, road access, daylight, and your own capabilities before traveling. Respect property restrictions and do not use the app while driving. They do not check for aurora changes, continuously monitor conditions, or promise a viewing opportunity. Open the app to refresh forecasts.

Notification permission, Focus settings, device availability, and iOS behavior can prevent or delay delivery. You can cancel a reminder in the app. The app cancels invalid reminders when it learns of expiration or revoked access; a refund or revocation may not be known while the app is offline. Do not rely on reminders for safety-critical decisions.
