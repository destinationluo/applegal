Privacy Policy

Spirit Box & EVP Recorder

Effective date and last updated: September 22, 2026

Spirit Box & EVP Recorder is an entertainment and audio-recording app. Your recordings, notes, word events, and magnetic readings are processed on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this content. No app account is required.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

This policy applies to Spirit Box & EVP Recorder (the “App”), provided by the developer identified on its App Store listing. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean that developer. For privacy questions or requests, email moguiris133@gmail.com.

2. Information used on your device

  • Microphone audio: With your permission, the App records surrounding sound when you start a recording or a microphone check. Recordings can include voices and other personal information. You can decline microphone access and use app-audio-only recording where available.
  • App audio: The App plays a built-in library of generated English words, voice fragments, and noise, and can save these sounds as a separate track. Your microphone audio is not sent to a speech, transcription, voice-cloning, or AI service.
  • Session information: The App stores recording files, titles, notes, bookmarks, dates, durations, playback events, sound settings, audio-route details, and free-session usage locally to support recording, recovery, playback, and export.
  • Magnetic readings: The App reads the device’s magnetometer where available and can save magnetic events during a session. It does not request GPS location or use these readings to determine your geographic location. Magnetic readings are not evidence of paranormal activity.
  • Preferences and purchases: The App uses local preferences and Apple’s verified purchase information to remember settings, track free-session usage, and enable Pro features.

The App has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information or use your recordings to train AI models.

3. Permissions and background recording

Microphone permission is requested before accessing your microphone. You can change it in iOS Settings. Denying it prevents environment recording but does not prevent playing built-in sounds. A Pro session that you start may continue recording while the screen is locked or the App is in the background; pause or end the session to stop recording. Free sessions pause when the App leaves the foreground. Opening a privacy page, share sheet, or Apple purchase flow can involve the corresponding system service or website.

4. Storage, backups, and deletion

Recordings and session records are stored in the App’s local storage. We do not provide an app account, a developer-hosted backup, or in-app cloud synchronization. Depending on your device and backup settings, Apple’s device backup or transfer services may include App data. Such copies are managed by Apple and your settings, not by an App cloud service.

You can delete saved sessions in History. Deleting the App removes its local container; offloading the App may retain its data. Exported or shared copies and device backups must be managed separately. Deleting a recording does not restore a used free session or delete Apple’s purchase records. Export important recordings before deleting the App or changing devices; we cannot recover recordings from a developer server.

The five-second microphone check uses a temporary local sample, which the App attempts to remove when you delete it, start a new check, or leave the check. Temporary export and playback files may remain in the App’s temporary storage until cleaned up by the App or iOS. This policy does not promise immediate removal of every cached or backed-up copy.

5. Exports, sharing, and support

Export files are created on your device. They are sent to another app, person, or storage provider only when you choose a destination in the system share interface. An export may include audio, your title and notes, event times, words, magnetic readings, and technical recording information, depending on the export format. Review the contents before sharing. The destination handles files under its own policies.

The help screen lets you preview and copy support information, including the App version, iOS version, device class, sound-check results, and any description you enter. Copying puts that information on the system clipboard; it does not send an email. The App does not automatically attach recordings, notes, word history, or device identifiers to a support request.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any files or details you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate the issue, and handle privacy requests. Our email provider processes those messages to deliver and store them. We retain support correspondence only as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or recordings of other people without permission.

6. Apple services and in-app purchases

Apple processes purchases, payment information, refunds, and purchase restoration through the App Store and StoreKit. The App checks Apple-provided transaction information, such as product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, and revocation status, to enable Pro. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password and do not send App transaction records to a developer-operated server.

Apple may process App Store usage, device diagnostics, or crash information under its own settings and policies, including diagnostics you choose to share with developers. This is separate from an analytics or recording-upload service within the App. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. This website and external links

This policy and our Terms are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting these pages sends a normal web request to GitHub. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. These pages contain no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data-collection information. Apple, GitHub, email providers, and destinations you choose may process information in other countries under their own policies.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can decline or revoke microphone permission, stop recording, delete sessions, choose whether to share files or contact support, and manage device backups in system settings. We cannot access or delete recordings held only on your device or copies you shared elsewhere. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other handling of personal information we hold, such as support correspondence. Contact us using the address above. You may also contact your local data-protection authority where applicable.

9. Children and security

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. The App relies on iOS app isolation and device security; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and use care when sharing recordings.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s data practices or applicable requirements change. We will update the date on this page and provide additional notice or request consent where required. See also our Terms of Use.
