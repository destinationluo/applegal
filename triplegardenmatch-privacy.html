Triple Garden Match Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Triple Garden Match is a casual puzzle game. We use limited information to run gameplay, remember local progress, provide online ranking or battle features when available, show rewarded ads, prevent fraud, and improve stability.

1. Who We Are

Triple Garden Match is provided by the app developer listed for the app in Apple App Store Connect. In this policy, "we", "us", and "our" refer to that app developer.

2. Information We Process

  • Local gameplay data: Level progress, score, high score, settings, hot update version, and similar game state may be stored on your device so the game can continue working between sessions.
  • Online game data: If online ranking or battle features are enabled, we may process gameplay identifiers, open ID or session tokens provided by the game platform, nickname, avatar URL, score, rank, battle result, and related gameplay records.
  • Advertising data: The app uses Unity LevelPlay, IronSource, Unity Ads, and related ad services to load and show rewarded ads. These services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers when permitted by iOS, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, ad performance, and fraud prevention signals.
  • Tracking permission status: On iOS, the app may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before initializing advertising services. Your choice controls whether the advertising identifier can be used for tracking as defined by Apple.
  • Diagnostics: Apple and third-party SDKs may provide crash, performance, device, and log information that helps us troubleshoot issues and improve app stability.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide game features, save progress, and maintain local settings.
  • To support ranking, battle, scoring, or similar online gameplay features when they are available.
  • To load, display, measure, cap, and reward ads.
  • To detect abuse, protect ad quality, prevent fraud, and keep the service secure.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve app performance.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that process data under their own privacy policies and platform rules, including Apple App Store services, Unity LevelPlay, IronSource, Unity Ads, and mediated advertising partners. These services may receive only the information needed to provide app distribution, diagnostics, advertising, ad measurement, ad quality, and fraud prevention services.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, camera recordings, microphone recordings, health data, or financial information through the game.
  • We do not require you to create an account with us to play the local puzzle game.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device unless you delete it or uninstall the app. Online gameplay, ranking, advertising, and diagnostics data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant feature, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Retention periods may vary by third-party service.

7. Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete local game data by deleting the app from your device.
  • You can control tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can limit personalized ads through Apple device settings where available.
  • You can contact us to request access, deletion, or correction of personal information we control, subject to applicable law and identity verification.

8. Children

Triple Garden Match is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in the country or region where you live and in other countries or regions where we or our service providers operate. We take reasonable steps to protect information according to this policy and applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, third-party services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at support@parentinglab.app.
